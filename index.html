FAQ

Straight answers.

What freight modes does NexHaul support?

The concept presents full truckload, LTL, expedited, drayage, cross-border, and managed transportation. A real operator should publish mode-specific capabilities and exclusions.

How would carriers join the network?

The carrier form collects authority, equipment, lane, fleet, and contact details. A production operation should then verify authority, insurance, safety, and payment information.

Does the concept include shipment tracking?

Yes. The interface demonstrates milestone updates and shared visibility. Production tracking would require integration with real transportation and location data.

Can the workflow support recurring lanes and spot freight?

Yes. The shipper inquiry supports recurring, seasonal, and one-time freight so the commercial team can qualify the request appropriately.

How would claims and service exceptions be handled?

A real operation should publish a documented escalation and claims process with required documents, named support, and response expectations. This concept intentionally does not invent those terms.

Is NexHaul a real freight company?

No. NexHaul Logistics is a fictional portfolio concept. Its people, operating data, contact details, and business claims are illustrative and should be replaced before a commercial launch.
