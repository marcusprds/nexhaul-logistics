Inclusive experience

Accessibility

Last reviewed August 30, 2026

This portfolio concept aims to provide a clear experience for keyboard, touch, screen-reader, zoom, and reduced-motion users.

Features included

  • Semantic headings, regions, forms, labels, and native disclosure controls.
  • A skip link and visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • A keyboard-operable mobile menu and inquiry tabs.
  • Inline form errors linked to their controls.
  • Content that remains available if animation JavaScript fails.
  • Reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.
  • Color contrast improvements for orange surfaces and small text.

Known limitations

Form responses are processed by Google Forms, a third-party service with its own accessibility behavior. Automated tools and code review cannot identify every real-world usability issue. The site should receive manual testing with current screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, browser zoom, high-contrast settings, and representative mobile devices before production use.

Feedback

The concept does not have a monitored accessibility contact. A production organization should publish a real email or telephone route, the formats available on request, and a response target for accessibility feedback.

Ongoing responsibility

Accessibility is not a one-time certification. Content, forms, integrations, images, and third-party services should be reviewed whenever the site changes.
