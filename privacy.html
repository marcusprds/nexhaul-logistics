Privacy notice

Last updated August 24, 2026

Important: NexHaul Logistics is fictional. This static portfolio demonstration does not transmit, store, or process information entered into its forms.

Information entered into forms

The inquiry forms validate information inside your browser and then display a demonstration success state. No form endpoint is configured, so names, email addresses, shipment details, carrier identifiers, and messages are not sent anywhere.

Cookies and analytics

This demonstration does not set marketing cookies, use analytics, or load advertising technology. If analytics or embedded third-party services are added later, this notice and any consent controls should be updated before publication.

Local browser behavior

The site uses JavaScript for navigation, form tabs, validation, animation, and the current copyright year. It does not intentionally write form entries to local storage, cookies, or browser databases.

Portraits and creative assets

Team portraits and branded assets are included as fictional portfolio content. A commercial operator must confirm the right to use every portrait, logo, testimonial, and customer mark before launch.

Before connecting a real form

  • Name the real data controller and contact details.
  • Describe the form provider, CRM, hosting, retention period, and access controls.
  • Publish lawful-use and deletion-request procedures.
  • Add jurisdiction-specific disclosures and consent language where required.

Questions
