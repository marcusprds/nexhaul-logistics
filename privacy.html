Privacy notice

Last updated August 30, 2026

Important: NexHaul Logistics is fictional. Information submitted through its inquiry forms is sent to a Google Form owned by the project owner.

Information entered into forms

Depending on the inquiry type, the forms may collect names, email addresses, company details, shipment origins and destinations, pickup dates, service preferences, carrier identifiers, preferred lanes, and messages. Required fields are identified within each form.

How submissions are processed

The website sends completed inquiries to Google Forms. Responses are stored in the form owner's Google account, and Google Forms sends the owner an email notification for each new response. Retention and deletion are controlled by the form owner. Google may process submission and technical information according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set marketing cookies, use analytics, or load advertising technology. Google Forms may use its own cookies and technical controls when processing a submission. If additional third-party services are added later, this notice and any consent controls should be updated before publication.

Local browser behavior

The site uses JavaScript for navigation, form tabs, validation, animation, and the current copyright year. It does not intentionally write form entries to local storage, cookies, or browser databases.

Portraits and creative assets

Team portraits and branded assets are included as fictional portfolio content. A commercial operator must confirm the right to use every portrait, logo, testimonial, and customer mark before launch.

Before a commercial launch

  • Name the real data controller and contact details.
  • Publish a retention schedule and deletion-request procedure.
  • Add appropriate access controls, spam protection, and a secure carrier-onboarding workflow.
  • Add jurisdiction-specific disclosures and consent language where required.
